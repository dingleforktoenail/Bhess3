How to Play Bhess3

Party Mode

Create a party to generate a 6-character room code. Share this code with a friend so they can join your room. Both players will play in real-time synced via secure room updates.

Draws & Resignations

When offering a draw or resigning, your opponent receives an interactive prompt instantly. Victories and defeats update stats for both players.

Chess Controls

Click any of your pieces to highlight legal move squares, then click a highlighted destination square to make your move.
